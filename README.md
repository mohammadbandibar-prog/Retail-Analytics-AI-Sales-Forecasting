# Retail Analytics & AI-Powered Sales Forecasting System

## 📌 Project Overview

The Retail Analytics & AI-Powered Sales Forecasting System is a data analytics project developed to analyze retail sales data and generate useful business insights.

The project analyzes revenue, stores, products, categories, customers, promotions, holidays, and seasonal sales patterns. It also performs store segmentation using K-Means clustering and forecasts future monthly revenue using time-series forecasting.

An interactive Streamlit dashboard is included to visualize the analysis, forecasting results, and business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze retail sales performance across different stores and product categories.
- Identify sales patterns, trends, and seasonal variations.
- Analyze revenue and sales performance by store, region, product, and brand.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Analyze the impact of promotions and holidays on sales.
- Segment stores based on their performance and characteristics.
- Forecast future monthly revenue using time-series forecasting.
- Develop an interactive dashboard to present data-driven business insights.

## 🔮 Revenue Forecasting

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis of the retail sales data produced several important business insights:

- The business generated approximately **₹41.13 Billion in total revenue** during the analyzed period.
- **Delhi** was the highest-revenue store among the eight stores.
- **Electronics** generated the highest revenue among the five product categories.
- **Returning customers** contributed a larger share of revenue compared with new customers.
- **August** was the strongest month when monthly revenue was aggregated across both years.
- **February** recorded the lowest aggregated monthly revenue.
- Revenue performance across the stores was relatively close, but clustering identified two groups with different performance characteristics.
- The Seasonal Naive forecasting model achieved a **2.60% MAPE** on the selected test period.
- The analysis of promotions and holidays uses normalized measures such as revenue per transaction to provide more meaningful comparisons.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis and forecasting results, the following recommendations can help support better retail decision-making:

### 1. Focus on High-Performing Stores

Identify the strategies and operational practices of high-performing stores and consider applying successful practices to lower-performing stores.

### 2. Improve Customer Retention

Returning customers generate a significant share of revenue. Customer loyalty programs and personalized offers can help improve customer retention.

### 3. Optimize Seasonal Planning

Use historical monthly sales patterns and forecasts to improve inventory planning, staffing, and promotional activities during high- and low-demand periods.

### 4. Evaluate Promotion Effectiveness

Promotions should be evaluated using normalized metrics such as revenue per transaction instead of relying only on total revenue.

### 5. Use Revenue Forecasts for Planning

Future revenue forecasts can support inventory management, budgeting, staffing, and sales planning.

### 6. Monitor Store Segments

Different store segments can be monitored separately and provided with appropriate strategies based on their performance characteristics.
